# Phone Actions and Switch — v1.1

**Status: final, in daily use.**

For Google Phone (`com.google.android.dialer`) and Google Contacts (`com.google.android.contacts`).

iOS VoiceOver-style list actions, brought to Android. Swipe right or left to move entry to entry without landing on the buttons inside each entry. Swipe down or up to cycle the entry's actions (Call, Message, Video call, Email, and so on), and double-tap to run the one you picked — a plain double-tap on a call-log entry calls. A three-finger swipe left or right switches between Recents and Contacts.

Download `phone-actions-switch.lua` and import it into SandFly.
